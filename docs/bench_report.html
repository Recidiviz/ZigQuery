GoogleSQL engine benchmark — consolidated

The 22-query goccy/googlesqlite corpus across five GoogleSQL implementations: the January 2026 BigQuery-emulator survey, goccy/googlesqlite v0.3.1's own published results, and zigquery measured with zig build gsql-bench.

Compiled 30 July 2026 · zigquery run on Apple M4 Pro, macOS 26.5.2, Zig 0.16.0, SQLite 3.51.0, ReleaseFast

Methodology

Read this before quoting a ratio

These numbers come from three different measurement contexts — different machines, harnesses, and layers of the stack. They are pooled here to show orders of magnitude, not to settle a head-to-head. A controlled comparison means re-running all five on one machine.

  • Context A — January 2026 emulator survey: goccy v0.6.6, recidiviz (cgo), goccy v0.7.0 (wasm). Internally comparable to each other.
  • Context B — goccy/googlesqlite v0.3.1 bench/RESULTS.md, published by upstream. Driver-level, prepared once and re-scanned per iteration.
  • Context C — zigquery, engine-level (no HTTP server), this repo's harness. The plotted full column re-analyzes every iteration, so it carries work the Context B figure does not.

zigquery against the January leader

recidiviz/bigquery-emulator (cgo) recorded the lowest time on 18 of 21 queries in the January survey. This is the ratio of its time to zigquery's full time, on a log axis centred at parity.

zigquery faster zigquery slower

The three scalar losses are the whole story of the deficit: they are dominated by per-statement GoogleSQL analysis, which the cgo emulator amortizes behind a prepared statement. Against zigquery's own prep column the same three run 13.5×, 16.3× and 30.5× faster than the cgo emulator. Cross-context — different machines.

Notes

  • Two queries do not run on zigquery. information_schema_columns needs INFORMATION_SCHEMA views, and wildcard_table_scan needs a wildcard table registered in the catalog. Both are BigQuery surface features that live in the emulator layer, not the translation engine — a scope boundary rather than a missing translation. They stay in the corpus and report as not-run so the suite doubles as a coverage signal.
  • Two queries legitimately return no rows. In priced_orders_report every generated orders.placed_at falls before the query's 2024-02-01 cutoff; in q5_local_supplier no lineitem row satisfies s.s_nationkey = c.c_nationkey across all 3,000 generated rows. Both still perform the full join and aggregate work. This is a property of upstream's generated data and affects every engine equally.
  • googlesqlite v0.3.1 differs from its main branch. The tagged release reports q3 at 32.65 s where main reports 33.23 s; v0.3.1's figures are used throughout.
  • zigquery's two columns bracket upstream's methodology. full runs the whole pipeline per iteration (analyze → transform → sqlite3_prepare → step → decode); prep compiles once and re-executes but discards rows. Upstream prepares once and scans, so its equivalent sits between them. full is plotted throughout as the conservative choice.
  • Reproduce: zig build gsql-bench -Drelease=true -- --phases. The corpus is vendored verbatim at testdata/bench_corpus/; the runner is harness/gsql_bench.zig.
